Privacy Policy Overview

Walkout Watch ("we", "our", "us") operates walkoutwatch.com.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Site. By accessing Walkout Watch, you consent to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Timezone Selection — Stored locally on your device (not sent to servers)
  • Contact Information — Email/message if you contact us
  • Cookies Consent — Your consent choices

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device info (browser, OS, device type)
  • Browsing activity (pages, time spent, referrer URLs)
  • IP address (standard web server logs)
  • Approximate location (based on IP geolocation)

1.3 Third-Party Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand user behavior. Google may collect device identifiers, browsing patterns, and approximate location.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use data to:

  • Improve Site functionality and user experience
  • Analyze traffic and usage patterns
  • Detect and prevent fraud
  • Respond to inquiries
  • Comply with legal obligations
What We DO NOT do:
  • Sell personal data to third parties
  • Share emails with affiliate partners
  • Send marketing emails without opt-in
  • Combine data with other sources without consent
  • Use data for purposes outside this policy

3. Affiliate Partners & Third-Party Sharing

When you click affiliate links, partners may collect their own data:

  • Streaming: Paramount+, ESPN+, DAZN, UFC Fight Pass
  • Sportsbooks: DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Bet365
  • Other: Amazon Prime Video and others
Important: We are not responsible for third-party data practices. Review their privacy policies before providing information.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Functional Cookies — Timezone, site settings (stored locally)
  • Analytics Cookies — Google Analytics (traffic data)
  • Third-Party Cookies — Affiliate partners may set cookies

You can disable cookies in browser settings, though this may affect functionality.

5. Data Retention

  • Timezone preference: Stored indefinitely on your device (you can clear anytime)
  • Analytics data: Retained per Google's policy (typically 14 months)
  • Server logs: Retained 30-90 days for security

6. Data Security

We implement standard security measures:

  • HTTPS encryption for all traffic
  • Secure GitHub Pages hosting
  • Regular backups
Disclaimer: No online transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Walkout Watch is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware of such data, we will delete it promptly.

Gambling content is restricted to 18+ users. Parents: use parental controls to restrict access.

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect
  • Delete your information
  • Opt-out of data sales (we don't sell data)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising rights

To exercise: Contact us with "CCPA Request" in the subject line.

9. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

EU residents have the right to:

  • Access personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or withdraw consent
  • Data portability

To exercise: Contact us with "GDPR Request" in the subject line.
