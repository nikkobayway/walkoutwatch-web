How We Stay Free (& How We Make Money)

Walkout Watch is free to use. We don't charge for timers, schedules, or timezone conversion.

We make money through partnerships with streaming services (DAZN, ESPN+, Paramount+) and sportsbooks (DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM). When you click a link to watch or place a bet, we earn a small commission — at no cost to you.

This means:

  • We have motivation to stay accurate and reliable (if our times are wrong, nobody clicks our links)
  • We're incentivized to keep the site fast and clean (complicated sites don't convert)
  • We can invest in new features without charging you

We're transparent about this. All affiliate links are clearly marked. Read our full disclaimer here.
